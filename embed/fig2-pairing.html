Pairing series one-to-one: recall, precision and swapped labels

Almost every benchmark starts the same way: each digitized series is paired with at most one ground-truth series by a minimum-cost assignment, and the summed similarity is divided by a count. Divide by the number of ground-truth series and you have recall; by the number of digitized series, precision; by the larger of the two, the CHART-Info 6b convention, which charges for both missing and invented curves. Where curves cross, a second question appears: are the values right but attached to the wrong legend label?

Figure 2. Three ground-truth curves (blue) and their digitizations (orange, dashed). The matrix on the right holds the pairwise similarity of every digitized line with every ground-truth line; the purple cells are the one-to-one assignment. The cards divide the same matched total by different counts, and the last card compares a label-aware score with a values-only score.

What to try

  • Tick Add a stray line P4. Recall does not move: the stray line is simply left unassigned. Precision and the 6b-style score fall, because a fourth digitized line now sits in the denominator. This is the difference between a metric that only asks “did you find my curves?” and one that also asks “did you invent any?”.
  • Tick Drop P3. Now recall and the 6b score fall while precision stays high: the two curves that were traced are traced well.
  • Tick Swap labels after the crossing. The values at every x are still correct, so the values-only score barely changes, but the label-aware score drops to about one half, because each digitized line follows the wrong ground-truth curve beyond the crossing. The difference between the two cards is the “swapping error” that FairChart2Table isolates (its SES is a values-only F1 minus a label-aware F1).
  • Raise the noise. All scores fall together; the assignment stays the same until the noise is large enough to confuse neighbouring curves.

How it is computed

Pairwise similarity used here: Sᵢⱼ = max(0, 1 − RMSE(Gᵢ, Pⱼ) ÷ y-range), RMSE on 200 shared x positions Assignment: X = one-to-one pairing maximizing Σ Sᵢⱼ Xᵢⱼ (Hungarian / exhaustive here) Recall = Σ S X ÷ Nᴳᵀ Precision = Σ S X ÷ Nᵖʳᵉᵈ CHART-Info 6b style = Σ S X ÷ max(Nᴳᵀ, Nᵖʳᵉᵈ) Label-aware (RMS style) = mean over ground-truth series of S between the series and the digitized line that carries its label Values-only (RNSS style) = at each x, sort the ground-truth values and the digitized values and compare them in order; average 1 − mean |Δ|

Notes on this implementation

  • CHART-Info scores each pair with its own continuous metric (Figure 2 of the first explorer); FPC-NRMSE pairs lines on min(e, 1) + (1 − coverage). The range-normalized RMSE used here keeps the arithmetic visible; the pairing and the denominators are what this figure is about.
  • The official CHART-Info task pages state that both Task 6a and Task 6b divide by max(#ground-truth, #predicted). LineFormer describes 6a as dividing by the number of ground-truth lines; the first explorer followed LineFormer. The “Recall” card here corresponds to that reading, the “6b style” card to the official text.
  • The stray line P4 carries no legend label, so it is excluded from the label-aware and values-only cards, which score only the three labelled series.
  • Curves are synthetic: two straight lines that cross at x ≈ 0.54 and a low wavy line.
